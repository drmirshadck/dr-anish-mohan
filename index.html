Dr. Anish Mohan trained at Government Homoeopathic Medical College, Calicut, and founded Homoeocare Multi Speciality Clinic in Manjakkal, Cherpulassery, in 2015. Eleven years on, the clinic has grown into a full team serving families across Palakkad.

His consultations are unhurried and detailed. Each one begins with a full case history so that treatment is individual to the patient. The online clinic brings that same approach to patients who cannot travel to Cherpulassery.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need to install an app?

No. The consultation opens in your browser on this website. Chrome or Safari on a phone or laptop works best.

How do I receive the consultation link?

After you pay and enter your UPI reference, the clinic verifies the payment and sends your link to the WhatsApp number you entered.

Can I consult from outside India?

Yes. Book the same way. If you can't pay by UPI, message the clinic on WhatsApp for other payment options.

How will I get my medicines?

After the consultation, the clinic arranges dispatch to your address. Delivery time depends on your location.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, free of charge up to 4 hours before your booked time. Message the clinic on WhatsApp to change your slot.
